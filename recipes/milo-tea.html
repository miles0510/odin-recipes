Milo Tea

Above is a Ghanaian natural cocoa processed powder used to prepare tea

INGREDIENT

  • sachete of milo
  • Milk
  • sugar
  • eggs
  • bread

STEPS

  1. boil water
  2. pour sachete of milo into a mug
  3. add a little sugar (optional)
  4. add milk
  5. add the hot boiled water
  6. stir evenly till mixed
  7. fry you eggs
  8. toast up your bread
  9. enjoy your meal....
