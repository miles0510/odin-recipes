Beans Stew Recipe

Beans Stew

The meal shown above is a locally Ghanaian dish (beans stew)

INGREDIENT

  • beans
  • oil
  • fresh tomatoes
  • tomato paste
  • spices (ginger, garlic, onions, pepper, external sachet spices)
  • Gizzards
  • Chicken

STEPS

  1. boil up beans till tender
  2. steam up gizzards and chicken
  3. heat up some amount of oil in a saucepan
  4. add up some onions
  5. chop your tomatoes and add to oil
  6. stir up a bit and add your tomato paste
  7. let it cook for 7 mins and add up your spices
  8. stir up for sometime and add your meat stock
  9. let it cook for 10 mins and add your cooked beans
  10. stir evenly and let it cook
  11. shallow fry your gizzard and chicken
  12. add the fried protein to stew and stir up well
  13. let it cook for 10mins
  14. enjoy your meal with rice, yam or any desired choice
